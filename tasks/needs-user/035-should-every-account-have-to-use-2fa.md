## Goal

Decide whether two-factor becomes required now the login is public, and what that means for a
household member who has only a phone — who cannot enrol today, because enrolment is web-only.

## Done when

- [ ] the answers are under Notes, and the work they ask for is filed as tasks

## Waiting on you

1. **Should 2FA be required on every account**, or stay optional?
2. If required: should the **phone get 2FA enrolment** — today it is website-only, so a household
   member who owns only a phone could not comply — or are phone-only accounts exempt?

## Notes

Filed from the survey of the otter, nautilus, nudge and dnd-near boards on 2026-10-26.

Source: dnd-near 190 and its D18 amendment: an environment variable pins the requirement on, and a
signed-in session that has not enrolled can still reach the enrolment routes.

- Enrolment is website-only (`wiki/Accounts-and-2FA.md`, end of "Redeeming one on a phone"), and
  CLAUDE.md §10 keeps it there. Requiring 2FA for a phone-only member means revisiting that rule or
  exempting them.
